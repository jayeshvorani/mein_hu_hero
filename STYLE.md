# Mein hu Hero: script house style

How the script is written in the editor, so every scene reads the same on
the site, in the PDF and in rehearsal. The numbers are referred to in
review notes ("rule 2").

## 1. Scene shape

- The Narrator speaks first, if the scene has narration.
- Next comes a stage direction labelled **Setting**: the stage picture at
  lights up (where we are, who is on, what they are doing). If time has
  passed, say so here ("Some years later.").
- Then the action.
- The show’s last row is the direction "The End."

## 2. Lines

- A line holds only the words the character says.
- A short delivery note may go in italic brackets, lower case, exactly
  where it applies: _(loudly)_, _(to Sumitra Devi)_, _(long pause)_. In the
  editor, type it in brackets and make it italic.
- Anything longer than about six words, or anything another character
  does, is its own stage direction row.
- A line never holds a song, a sound or a request for one.
- The speaker is the character’s name only. Their role is in the cast
  list. Age and time changes go in the scene’s Setting.

## 3. Stage directions

- Present tense, third person, full sentences, ending with a full stop:
  "Raj enters. Sumitra Devi performs the aarti."
- Call characters by their cast-list name, not "Mother", "Father" or a
  title.
- No brackets and no italics: the site and the PDF already set directions
  in grey italics.
- Label **Setting** for a scene’s opening picture, and for a change of
  place within a scene ("Inside Deendayal’s office."). Leave the label
  blank otherwise.

## 4. Music and sound

- Music and sound are only ever song or sound-effect rows, placed where
  the music or sound starts, before the action it accompanies.
- A direction does not repeat the cue next to it ("the music changes").
- A direction may name a song when the action depends on it: the title in
  quotes with the song highlight.
- The sound highlight is only for words describing a sound, never for an
  action.

## 5. Requests for audio

- When the audio is not made yet, add a Song or Sound effect row and
  describe what is needed in its box, with any reference (links included).
  Only the audio goes in the box: what the actors do is a direction.
- The note under a cue is for the sound operator’s timing ("fade out when
  Raj enters"), and the cast sees it. It is not for "coming soon".

## 7. Hinglish spelling

text it. Long vowels are doubled only where the word is always written
that way (aaj, aap, aao, maa, naam, baad).

appears, including when the Narrator says it.

## 8. The running gag

Once in each of scenes 1 to 7: the direction "Raj puts his hand to his
forehead." followed by the "Fail 2" sound effect. In the finale Raj breaks
the habit: his hand starts towards his forehead, but he shrugs it off and
dances, and there is no sound effect.

## 9. Optional lines

Mark a line or direction optional (yellow on the site, a gold edge in the
PDF) only when it can be cut if the show runs long. Nothing else uses that
mark.

## Scene details

- **Location:** the place’s name, plus a short note only when the scene
  uses it differently.
- **Props:** the prop’s name, then a lower-case note if needed ("rolled
  paper tied with a ribbon").
- **Set changes:** one instruction per step, in the imperative, with a
  full stop ("Take the covers off the home chairs."). Something set before
  the show goes under scene 1, starting "Before the show".
